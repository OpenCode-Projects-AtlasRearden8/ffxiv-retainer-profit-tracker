FFXIV Retainer Profit Tracker

Track retainer venture sales and marketboard reselling profit — manual entries only, all data stays in your browser.

Log a sale

Enter one completed sale per row. Buy price is what you paid per item; sell price is what your retainer listed and sold it for. No scraping here — just your own numbers, typed in.

Overall summary

Tax is calculated as a percentage of the sale proceeds (rounded to the nearest gil), so net = sell total − tax and profit = net − buy total.

Per-retainer summaries

Best-margin rankings

Ranked by profit margin (% profit on the gil you spent). Entries where you paid nothing (e.g. from your own ventures or gardens) are excluded because a margin would be meaningless.

Weekly profit chart


    
Weeks start on Monday. Plain numbers only: each block is 1/40th of your best week, so text readers and screen readers get the same picture.

      Tax presets: where these numbers come from
      
Market tax rates in FFXIV differ by data center and change over time as players adjust rates in each of the three main cities. The presets below are best-effort starting defaults and may be out of date — check your server’s current market tax ingame and use the “Custom” option if it differs. You can also edit presets here.
